Privacy

Voisu has no account, no server and no analytics. Your audio goes only to the speech providers you configure, with your own keys. Everything Voisu keeps stays on your computer unless you export it yourself.

What leaves your computer

Sent toWhatWhen
DeepgramYour audio, streamedWhile a Recording runs, unless you've turned Deepgram off (Linux)
GroqYour audio, then the Source Transcript text for reconciliation and formattingWhen you stop a Recording
GitHubAn update check (Windows)At start and every 6 hours

Nothing is sent before you start a Recording. If you cancel one, streaming stops and nothing is delivered. What the providers do with your requests is governed by their own terms and privacy policies, on groq.com and deepgram.com.

With Local transcription on Linux, the model runs on your computer and your audio doesn't leave it. See Local transcription.

What stays on your computer

  • API keys live in your OS keyring: Windows Credential Manager, or the Secret Service keyring on Linux.
  • Logs are written locally and never uploaded. On Windows they're in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Voisu\logs.
  • Last transcript on Windows is held in memory only. It is never written to disk and is gone when Voisu quits.
  • Local history on Linux keeps up to the last 200 Recordings or 7 days, whichever comes first, so voisu history and voisu export can help you diagnose problems. Saved text is capped at 8 KB per entry.
  • Audio isn't saved by default. A debug capture is kept only when you explicitly turn one on, and expires after an hour.

Exports

voisu export <id> writes a redacted diagnostic export of a single Recording to your computer. It's yours to read, and it goes nowhere unless you attach it to a bug report.

The clipboard

On Windows, Voisu marks its paste so it stays out of clipboard history (Win V), cloud clipboard sync and clipboard monitors, then puts your previous clipboard back. On Linux the Transcript is placed on the clipboard to deliver it, and stays there.

Clearing what Voisu keeps

  • Linux history: stop the daemon and delete ~/.local/state/voisu/diagnostics.
  • Windows logs: quit Voisu and delete %LOCALAPPDATA%\Voisu\logs.
  • Keys: remove them from your keyring app or Credential Manager. See Uninstalling.
