Voisu docs

Voisu is voice dictation for Linux and Windows. You press your key, speak, and the Transcript lands in the window you were typing in.

New here? Start with Getting started: install, add your keys and make your first Recording. It takes a few minutes.

A few words we use

Recording
The audio captured between starting and stopping dictation.
Transcript
The final text Voisu produces from a Recording.
Trigger Key
On Linux, the shortcut you approved for Voisu. Its first press starts a Recording and the next press stops it. On Windows the gesture is Ctrl Win.
Delivery
Putting the Transcript into the app you're using. If that can't happen, the Transcript stays on your clipboard.
Overlay
The small capsule that shows Voisu is listening, working, or has hit a problem.

Two platforms, two gestures. On Windows you hold Ctrl Win while you speak and release to finish. On Linux you press the Trigger Key once to start and again to stop.
