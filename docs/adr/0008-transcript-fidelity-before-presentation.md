# Put Transcript Fidelity before presentation and latency

Voisu preserves the meaning and complete content of a Recording before optimizing presentation or latency. When these goals conflict, it may deliver uncertain or minimally formatted text, but it must not silently delete, invent, or reinterpret speech. This makes formatting optional and fail-closed, while keeping bounded Delivery subordinate to the existence of a fidelity-safe Source Transcript.
